# Rev D (12 V) change register vs Rev-C1

Baseline: github.com/hoeken/brineomatic tag **Rev-C1**. Envelope: ~3 A board (3.15 A time-lag fuse, 3 A per output), 12 V house bus (9-15 V; 14.8 V max charge voltage).
Goals: see `GOALS.md`. Complete machine-generated diff: `CHANGE_AUDIT.md` (step 25: 179 parts identical, 131 changed/added/removed, **0 unexplained**; 0 net differences involving a part that is not listed below).

**Evidence classes** used in the "Due diligence" column
- **LIVE** - checked on jlcpcb.com / lcsc.com in this project (stock, Basic/fee-free status, price).
- **CALC** - my own arithmetic, shown in `VETTING.md` or here.
- **DS** - number read from the manufacturer's datasheet PDF on 2026-10-31 (named in the cell); **DS?** = taken from a distributor page summary or from memory and still to be confirmed against the PDF.
- **SIM/BENCH** - none yet. **No bench test has been done (V6 open).**

Risk class: R0 none, R1 re-rating/value change, R2 different part same function, R3 function/architecture change.

## F. Manufacturing / assembly facts (JLCPCB Economic PCBA)

- Economic requires top-side parts only and qty 2 or 5. THT parts (sockets, terminals, fuse holder, headers, buzzer) are hand/wave soldered at extra cost ($3.61 hand-soldering + $7.97 manual assembly on the 5-board quote).
- The **socket BOM line comes up unselected** on JLCPCB ("multiple types of parts") and must be ticked manually; otherwise the sockets are not assembled.
- **CPL rules learnt the hard way:** (1) JLCPCB expects the *centre* of a part, KiCad's position file gives the pin-1 origin: the 3.5 mm terminals are written with the pin-row centre (`tools/make_jlc.py`); (2) the 1x22 socket rows need rotation 90, otherwise they lie horizontally in JLC's viewer; (3) THT parts checked in JLC's viewer on 2026-10-31: **BZ1, C21, C23, C57, L2 and the fuse holder F1 sit correctly** (F1 is written with the courtyard centre, `CENTRE` table in `tools/make_jlc.py`); the 2x3 headers J1/J6 that did not sit correctly no longer exist (D2). The four east-edge terminals (rotation 90, centre of the pin row) sit on their pads in the viewer. (4) **Rotation (step 24):** JLCPCB's library orientation differs from KiCad's for some packages; the CPL rotation is KiCad's + a per-package correction + the symbol's `Rotation Offset` field, as in Rev-C1's Fabrication-Toolkit output (`tools/make_jlc.py`, `REVIEW_FIXES.md`). Rules (1)-(3) were checked for position only; SMD orientation still has to be checked in the placement preview.
- Each extended line is charged $3.09 per order; 14 lines are charged ($43.26): C3342, C3349 (e-caps), C2687129 (PESD3V3L1BA), C5199854 (PSD12C), C70129 (mSMD260), C394425 (ferrites), C160390 (JST-SH), C177249 (L2), C728670 (R3/R41), C327071 (R57/R60), C37593 (ADS1115), C127866 (RT7272), C2649430 (MAX40200), C2150710 (TMC2209). Step 25 adds C1849461 (TPS16630PWPR, U3): 15 lines ($46.35).
